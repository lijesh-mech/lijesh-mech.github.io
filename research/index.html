Research

Developing thermodynamics-based theories, experimental methods, sensing technologies, and predictive tools for understanding degradation and improving the reliability of materials, machines, batteries, and manufacturing processes.

My research is organized around a common objective: transforming irreversible energy dissipation into measurable information about damage, degradation severity, failure, and remaining life. The six areas below represent the principal scientific and application domains of this work.

AREA 01

Thermodynamics-Based Degradation and Failure

Develops a unified thermodynamic framework for quantifying irreversible degradation, relating entropy generation to measurable damage, identifying failure thresholds, and estimating remaining useful life under constant and variable operating conditions. The framework spans mechanical, electrochemical, electrical, energy-storage, and manufacturing systems.
